# Allternit Research Publication Templates

This directory contains templates for publishing research at Allternit PBC.

## Available Templates

| Template | Use Case | Target Length |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| `research-paper.md` | Full research papers with peer review | 2,000-10,000 words |
| `economic-index.md` | Quarterly economic analysis reports | 2,000-4,000 words |
| `blog-post.md` | Research blog posts and summaries | 800-2,000 words |

## Quick Start

1. Copy the appropriate template
2. Fill in the YAML frontmatter
3. Write your content following the structure
4. Submit for review via the research workflow

## Publication Workflow

See [RESEARCH_PUBLICATION_WORKFLOW.md](/workflow) for the complete publication process.

## Style Guide

### Voice and Tone
- Professional but accessible
- Humble and transparent
- Mission-aligned with PBC purpose

### Formatting
- Use Markdown for all content
- Include proper YAML frontmatter
- Follow APA citation style
- Use Oxford commas

### Required Elements

All publications must include:
- Title and date
- Author(s) with affiliations
- Abstract/summary
- License (default: CC BY 4.0)
- Conflict of interest statement
